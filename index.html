Part 2 — READ: Start with a Personal Recount

Read this personal recount first. At this stage, focus only on a personal experience and how the events are organized. The local historical texts will appear in the Explore part.

My Unforgettable School Trip Last semester, my classmates and I went on a school trip to a beautiful natural park near our town. Our English teacher organized the trip to help us learn outside the classroom. We left school at seven o’clock in the morning by bus. Everyone felt excited because it was our first trip together that year. When we arrived, our teacher divided us into small groups. First, we walked along a nature trail and observed different plants and animals. Then, we worked together to complete a short observation task. At one point, my group took the wrong path, so we had to check the map and ask our teacher for directions. Fortunately, we found the correct path and continued our journey. After that, we had lunch together under some shady trees. We shared our food, told funny stories, and took pictures. Before going home, each group presented what they had learned during the trip. I really enjoyed the trip because I learned more about nature and the importance of teamwork. It also taught me to stay calm when facing problems. The experience was simple, but it became one of my most memorable school activities.

Read and Understand

1. Who organized the school trip?
2. What problem did the writer’s group face?
3. HOTS: Which statement best explains why the writer included the problem with the wrong path?

Part 3 — EXPLORE: Local Historical Recount

Now move from a personal experience to local heritage. Read the following texts and watch the video. Use only details stated in the materials or verified by a reliable source.

Text A — Saribu Rumah Gadang

Saribu Rumah Gadang is a well-known cultural tourism area in Solok Selatan. It is located in Koto Baru, Sungai Pagu. The area is known for its traditional Minangkabau houses, which stand close to one another and preserve the cultural identity of the local community. The popular name became more widely known in 2008, when Meutia Hatta Swasono visited Koto Baru. She saw many Rumah Gadang in a relatively close area and gave the place the nickname “Saribu Rumah Gadang.” Although the name means “a thousand Rumah Gadang,” the actual number of houses is not one thousand. Over time, the area developed as a tourism destination. Some Rumah Gadang were preserved as cultural heritage buildings, while the community also developed homestays, traditional food, crafts, and cultural performances. Today, the area helps visitors learn about Minangkabau architecture, traditions, and community life.

Text B — Istano Rajo Balun

Istano Rajo Balun is a local cultural heritage site associated with the traditional community of Balun in Solok Selatan. The word “istano” refers to a palace or a traditional residence connected with a ruler. The site represents the importance of local leadership, customary traditions, and the preservation of Minangkabau cultural identity. When learning about Istano Rajo Balun, readers should identify its location, its connection with local customary history, and the ways the community maintains its heritage. Specific dates, names, and historical events should be checked against reliable local sources before they are included in a historical account.

Text C — Masjid 60 Aso

Masjid 60 Aso is a local religious and cultural heritage site in Solok Selatan. As a mosque, it has a role in religious life and can also represent the history, craftsmanship, and community traditions of the area. Heritage buildings such as this can help younger generations understand how religious practice and local identity are connected. To write an accurate Historical Recount about Masjid 60 Aso, students should verify its exact location, the origin of its name, the date of construction, and important events in its history using trustworthy sources. Do not guess missing dates or details.
Source-awareness note: The short overview texts for Istano Rajo Balun and Masjid 60 Aso introduce their cultural significance but do not provide verified dates or a full chronology. If you include specific historical details in your work, verify them using a reliable source and record that source.

QAR means Question–Answer Relationship: Right There (stated directly), Think and Search (combine details), and Author and You (reason using the text and your own thinking). Choose one answer (A–E) for every question. Questions include higher-order thinking skills (HOTS).

1. (Right There) Where is Saribu Rumah Gadang located?
2. (Right There) What happened in 2008 according to Text A?
3. (Think and Search) Why is the name “Saribu Rumah Gadang” not a literal count?
4. (Think and Search) What do Istano Rajo Balun and Masjid 60 Aso have in common in the provided texts?
5. (HOTS — Evaluate) Which source practice is most responsible when writing about a site whose construction date is not given in the text?
6. (HOTS — Analyze) Which detail best shows that Saribu Rumah Gadang developed over time?
7. (Author and You — HOTS) A visitor posts incorrect information about a heritage site. What is the best response?
8. (Video Connection) Which note from the video can you support with a specific detail you observed or heard?
